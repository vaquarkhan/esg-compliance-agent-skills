## Sustainability assurance attestation

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Assurance status** | `pending_sustainability_assurance_sign_off` (default) or `sustainability_assurance_signed_off` |
| **Sustainability assuror** | Name, firm, credential (ISAE 3000 / AA1000) — required when signed off |
| **Signed at** | ISO-8601 timestamp when external assurance complete |
| **Scope** | e.g., GHG inventory FY2025, CSRD ESRS E1 datapoints |
| **Human review required** | `true` until assuror sign-off recorded |

> Repository maintainers may approve checklist text updates. **Only a qualified sustainability assuror** may change `assurance_status` to signed-off for filing-bound artifacts.
